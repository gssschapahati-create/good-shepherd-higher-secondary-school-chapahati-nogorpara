Employee State Insurance Corporation (ESIC) Registration

Important Instructions / Niamrang (Read Before Filling)

English:

  • Uploading documents and photos in this portal is optional. If you choose to upload, the file size MUST be between 20 KB and 50 KB.
  • However, all staff must submit HARD COPIES of the following to the School Office by Monday:
    • Your Aadhaar Card, PAN Card, and Bank Passbook.
    • Aadhaar Cards of all added Family Members.
    • Passport Photos of yourself and all added Family Members.
  • Hard copies are mandatory even if you upload them here.

Garo:

  • Ia portal-o document aro photo-rangko upload ka'aniara nangchongmota ong'ja (optional). Upload ka'genchimode file size 20 KB - 50 KB gisepo ong'na nanggen.
  • Indiba, pilak staff-rangko HARD COPY-rangko School Office-o Sombar salo on'echina ge'eta:
    • Na'ani Aadhaar Card, PAN Card, aro Bank Passbook.
    • Nokdangni manderangni (Family Members) Aadhaar Card-rang.
    • An'tangni aro pilak Nokdangni manderangni Passport Photo-rang.
  • Portal-o upload ka'oba ba ka'jaoba, hard copy-ko on'chongmotna nanggen.
